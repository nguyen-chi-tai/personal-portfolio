About me

I grew up in Vietnam. Before I wrote my first real program, I worked as a server at a wedding banquet restaurant and as a kitchen assistant. A busy kitchen taught me what I now look for in software: every station has one job, orders flow in a clear direction, and when one part fails the whole service should not collapse.

In high school I was a provincial-level excellent student in mathematics, and I spent a lot of time on combinatorics and probability problems. That habit of breaking a hard problem into small, countable pieces is the part of math I use most when I program.

I came to the United States, finished the Intensive English Program, and I'm now studying Computer Science at Southeast Missouri State University in Cape Girardeau, Missouri.
